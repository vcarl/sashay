Sashay 1.0: a sliding-pane layout for the web. Lay out panes side by side, let them slide over one another as space runs out, fold overflowing panes into a spine, and collapse to a tab strip on narrow screens. Ships a framework-agnostic controller (`createSashay`), a React binding (`@vcarl/sashay/react`), and a small stylesheet (`@vcarl/sashay/style.css`) themed through `--sashay-*` CSS properties.
