Every edge takes focus. An edge with a divider is a `role="separator"` that reports its position with `aria-valuenow` and `aria-valuetext`. The first edge has no divider, so it is a `role="button"` with `aria-expanded`. On a focused edge, ← and → move between snaps, and Enter or Space opens or closes the pane. No drag is ever required.

Drawer toggles and compact tabs sit beside the edge, not inside it, so screen readers announce them. Drawer toggles carry `aria-expanded`. Covered and off-screen panes are `inert`, so focus and screen readers skip what you can't see. The `title` you give each pane names its edge, its compact tab and its close button.
