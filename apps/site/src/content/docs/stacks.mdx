A stack is just an array. Picking an item adds a pane on the right; going back removes it. There are no push or pop helpers: add or remove the pane elements, then call `update({ panes })`. sashay diffs by `id`: a new pane slides in and opens to its `focus`; a removed pane closes, gives its width to its neighbour, then leaves.

When the open panes no longer fit, the oldest fold into one spine at the left with a count. Clicking it, or pressing ← on it, reveals the newest hidden pane. Closed panes never fold, so a fixed workspace that fits never shows a spine.
