## Houseplants, in three panes.

A fixed workspace. Each dot is a houseplant, placed by the sun it needs and how often it wants water. Click one to see its nearest neighbours, and click it again in the list for its card; the × closes it. Open the **Window** bar at the bottom of the chart to see which plants your window suits.

The chart is a fixed 720 by 440 pixels, so a narrow pane crops it. Squeeze the chart and the plant you picked stays in view.

## A house, all the way down.

A navigation stack. Open a room, then a piece of furniture, a drawer, a box. Each opens a pane; the back link, or any entry further left, takes you back. On a narrow frame the oldest levels fold into the spine.
