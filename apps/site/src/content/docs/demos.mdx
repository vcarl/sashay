## A portfolio, in three panes.

A fixed workspace. Open the **Interval** drawer on the left edge to change the range. Click a bar to see that period's transactions, then a transaction for its details; the × closes it. Squeeze the overview and the bar you picked stays in view.

<DemoApp name="finance" />

## A house, all the way down.

A navigation stack. Open a room, then a piece of furniture, a drawer, a box. Each opens a pane; the back link, or any entry further left, takes you back. On a narrow frame the oldest levels fold into the spine.
